### Data Cleaning/Preparation

- Reviewed missing values, duplicate IDs and data types.
- Checked date formats and categorical fields.
- Calculated revenue using Unit Price × Quantity.
- Extracted Day, Month and Year for time-based analysis.

### Exploratory Data Analysis

#### Analysed:

- Revenue and quantity sold.
- Sales performance by city and product.
- Monthly and yearly sales trends.
- Customer and transaction activity.
- Payment method distribution.

### Key Findings

- Chicken (1kg) emerged as the leading product by revenue in the dashboard.
- London ranked highest among the five leading cities by revenue.
- October recorded the highest monthly revenue in the displayed trend.
- Sales performance varied across months, cities and products.

### Business Recommendations

- Prioritise inventory planning around high-revenue products.
- Strengthen sales strategies in leading cities.
- Investigate monthly sales fluctuations to identify opportunities for growth.
- Monitor sales performance regularly using interactive reporting.

### Limitations

- The analysis is limited to the available dataset and its reporting period.
- Profit margins, operating costs and inventory levels were not provided.
- Revenue performance alone does not establish overall profitability.
